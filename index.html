EMBERWING

Hold to fly. Burn what blocks you. Dodge what won't burn.

How to play
  • Hold anywhere to beat your wings. Let go to dive.
  • FIRE burns wisps and crystals — but ember is limited.
  • Rune gates only open to fire. Save ember for them.
  • Stone spikes never burn. Those you dodge.
  • Purple gems refill ember and build your combo multiplier.
  • Fill the rage meter, then hit RAGE to smash through anything for five seconds.

Keyboard: Space fly · F fire · R rage
